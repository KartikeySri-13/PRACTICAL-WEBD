How it began

Three chairs, one filter, and a landlord who liked the smell

We opened Chill Point in a lane just off the main road, in a space barely big enough for the counter and a window seat. The first batch of filter coffee was for ourselves — the second was for whoever wandered in asking what smelled so good.

Everything here is built around slowness: coffee brewed in small batches, chai stirred by hand, and a room with nowhere you need to be. Regulars know the corner table by the plant. New faces usually end up there too, eventually.
